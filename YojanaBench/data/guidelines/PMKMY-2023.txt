GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Maandhan Yojana
SCHEME ID: PMKMY-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: PENSION
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A voluntary contributory pension scheme for small and marginal farmers that provides a assured monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after the age of 60.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMKMY-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be a farmer.
   [PMKMY-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be between 18 and 40 years of age.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMKMY-E1] The subscriber receives an assured monthly pension of Rs. 3000 after attaining the age of 60.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMKMY-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PMKMY-D2] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook
   [PMKMY-D3] (MANDATORY) Land Records

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMKMY-APL1] Level 1: Common Service Centre Nodal Officer - within 30 days. First grievance at the enrolment centre.
   [PMKMY-APL2] Level 2: State Nodal Officer - within 60 days. Escalation at state level.
   [PMKMY-APL3] Level 3: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare - within 90 days. Final grievance at the Ministry.
