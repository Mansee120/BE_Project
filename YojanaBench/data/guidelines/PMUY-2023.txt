GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana
SCHEME ID: PMUY-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: ENERGY
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   Provides a free LPG cooking gas connection, with the first refill and a stove, to adult women from Below Poverty Line (BPL) households so that clean cooking fuel replaces firewood and other unhealthy fuels.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMUY-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 18 years of age or older.
   [PMUY-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be a woman.
   [PMUY-C3] (MANDATORY) The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMUY-E1] An eligible woman receives a free LPG connection worth about Rs. 1600, including the security deposit, first refill and a stove.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMUY-D1] (MANDATORY) BPL Card
   [PMUY-D2] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PMUY-D3] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook
   [PMUY-D4] (MANDATORY) Address Proof

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMUY-APL1] Level 1: Block Development Officer (BDO) - within 30 days. First appeal at block level.
   [PMUY-APL2] Level 2: District Supply Officer - within 60 days. Second appeal at district level.
   [PMUY-APL3] Level 3: State Food Commission - within 90 days. Final appeal at state level.
