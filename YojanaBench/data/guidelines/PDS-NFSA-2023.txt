GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Public Distribution System (National Food Security Act, 2013)
SCHEME ID: PDS-NFSA-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: FOOD
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   Provides subsidised foodgrains to eligible households through Fair Price Shops under the National Food Security Act, 2013. Priority Households (PHH) and Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) families receive monthly entitlements.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PDS-C1] (MANDATORY) The household must hold a valid ration card classified as either Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) or Priority Household (PHH).
   [PDS-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be a resident of the State/UT where the ration card is issued.
   [PDS-C3] (MANDATORY) A household that pays income tax or is covered under a statutory income-tax category is excluded from Priority Household coverage.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PDS-E1] Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) households receive 35 kg of foodgrains per household per month.
   [PDS-E2] Priority Household (PHH) families receive 5 kg of foodgrains per person per month.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PDS-D1] (MANDATORY) Ration Card
   [PDS-D2] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PDS-D3] (MANDATORY) Residence/Address Proof

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PDS-APL1] Level 1: District Supply Officer (DSO) - within 30 days. First appeal against rejection or card correction at the district level.
   [PDS-APL2] Level 2: State Food Commission - within 60 days. Second appeal to the State Food Commission if the district decision is unsatisfactory.
