GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Atal Pension Yojana
SCHEME ID: APY-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: PENSION
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A guaranteed pension scheme for unorganised sector workers, providing a fixed monthly pension between Rs. 1000 and Rs. 5000 after the age of 60, depending on the contribution chosen.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [APY-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 18 years of age or older.
   [APY-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 40 years of age or younger.
   [APY-C3] (MANDATORY) The applicant must not be an income tax payer.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [APY-E1] The subscriber receives a guaranteed minimum monthly pension of Rs. 1000 after the age of 60.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [APY-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [APY-D2] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook
   [APY-D3] (MANDATORY) Mobile Number

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [APY-APL1] Level 1: Bank Nodal Officer - within 30 days. First grievance at the bank.
   [APY-APL2] Level 2: PFRDA Grievance Redressal Cell - within 60 days. Escalation to the pension regulator.
   [APY-APL3] Level 3: Pension Ombudsman - within 90 days. Final grievance before the Pension Ombudsman.
