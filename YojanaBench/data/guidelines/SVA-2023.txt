GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi)
SCHEME ID: SVA-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: LIVELIHOOD
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A micro-credit scheme that provides collateral-free working capital loans to urban street vendors to help them resume their livelihoods, with interest subsidy and digital cashback on repayments.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [SVA-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 18 years of age or older.
   [SVA-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be an urban street vendor.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [SVA-E1] An eligible street vendor receives a first working capital loan of Rs. 10000.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [SVA-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [SVA-D2] (MANDATORY) Letter of Recommendation from Urban Local Body
   [SVA-D3] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [SVA-APL1] Level 1: Urban Local Body Nodal Officer - within 30 days. First grievance at the urban local body.
   [SVA-APL2] Level 2: Municipal Commissioner - within 60 days. Escalation at the municipal level.
   [SVA-APL3] Level 3: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs - within 90 days. Final grievance at the Ministry.
