GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana
SCHEME ID: PMSBY-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: INSURANCE
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   An accidental death and disability insurance scheme offering cover of Rs. 2 lakh for accidental death or total disability and Rs. 1 lakh for partial disability, for a nominal annual premium.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMSBY-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 18 years of age or older.
   [PMSBY-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 70 years of age or younger.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMSBY-E1] Accidental death or total disability is covered up to Rs. 2 lakh.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMSBY-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PMSBY-D2] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook
   [PMSBY-D3] (MANDATORY) Consent cum Declaration Form

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMSBY-APL1] Level 1: Bank Branch Manager - within 15 days. First grievance at the bank branch.
   [PMSBY-APL2] Level 2: Scheme Nodal Officer (Bank) - within 30 days. Escalation to the bank's scheme nodal officer.
   [PMSBY-APL3] Level 3: Insurance Ombudsman - within 90 days. Final grievance before the Insurance Ombudsman.
