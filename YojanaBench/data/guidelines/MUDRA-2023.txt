GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana
SCHEME ID: MUDRA-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: CREDIT
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A scheme that provides collateral-free loans to non-farm micro and small enterprises through banks, NBFCs and microfinance institutions under the Shishu, Kishore and Tarun categories.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [MUDRA-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 18 years of age or older.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [MUDRA-E1] Loan limits are tiered: Shishu up to Rs. 50000, Kishore up to Rs. 5 lakh and Tarun up to Rs. 10 lakh, based on the applicant's annual income.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [MUDRA-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [MUDRA-D2] (MANDATORY) Business Plan
   [MUDRA-D3] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [MUDRA-APL1] Level 1: Bank Branch Manager - within 15 days. First grievance at the bank branch.
   [MUDRA-APL2] Level 2: Zonal Head - within 45 days. Escalation to the zonal office.
   [MUDRA-APL3] Level 3: Banking Ombudsman - within 90 days. Final grievance before the Banking Ombudsman.
