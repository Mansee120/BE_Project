GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan
SCHEME ID: PMKUSUM-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: ENERGY
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A solar energy scheme that provides subsidised standalone solar pumps and solarisation of existing agriculture pumps to farmers, giving reliable irrigation and an additional source of income.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMKUSUM-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be a farmer.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMKUSUM-E1] An eligible farmer receives a subsidy of up to Rs. 60000 towards the cost of a solar pump.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMKUSUM-D1] (MANDATORY) Land Records
   [PMKUSUM-D2] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PMKUSUM-D3] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook
   [PMKUSUM-D4] (MANDATORY) Electricity Bill

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMKUSUM-APL1] Level 1: DISCOM Officer - within 30 days. First grievance with the power distribution company.
   [PMKUSUM-APL2] Level 2: State Nodal Agency - within 60 days. Escalation to the state renewable energy agency.
   [PMKUSUM-APL3] Level 3: Ministry of New and Renewable Energy - within 90 days. Final grievance at the Ministry.
