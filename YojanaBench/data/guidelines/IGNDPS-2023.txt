GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Indira Gandhi National Disability Pension Scheme
SCHEME ID: IGNDPS-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: PENSION
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   Monthly pension for persons aged 18 years and above with severe or multiple disabilities (80% or more) belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [IGNDPS-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 18 years of age or older.
   [IGNDPS-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be below 80 years of age.
   [IGNDPS-C3] (MANDATORY) The applicant must have a disability of 80% or more.
   [IGNDPS-C4] (MANDATORY) The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [IGNDPS-E1] Eligible beneficiaries receive a pension of Rs. 300 per month.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [IGNDPS-D1] (MANDATORY) Disability Certificate (80% or more)
   [IGNDPS-D2] (MANDATORY) Age Proof (Birth Certificate/School Certificate)
   [IGNDPS-D3] (MANDATORY) BPL Card
   [IGNDPS-D4] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [IGNDPS-D5] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [IGNDPS-APL1] Level 1: Block Development Officer (BDO) - within 30 days. First appeal at block level.
   [IGNDPS-APL2] Level 2: District Social Welfare Officer - within 60 days. Second appeal at district level.
   [IGNDPS-APL3] Level 3: State Social Welfare Commission - within 90 days. Final appeal at state level.
