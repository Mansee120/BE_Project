GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Jal Jeevan Mission
SCHEME ID: JJM-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: WATER
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A mission to provide every rural household with a functional household tap connection supplying adequate and safe drinking water in usable quantity on a regular basis.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [JJM-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must live in a rural area.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [JJM-E1] Every eligible rural household receives an in-kind functional household tap water connection; there is no cash benefit.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [JJM-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [JJM-D2] (MANDATORY) Address Proof

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [JJM-APL1] Level 1: Gram Panchayat - within 15 days. First grievance at the village level.
   [JJM-APL2] Level 2: Block Development Officer (BDO) - within 30 days. Escalation at block level.
   [JJM-APL3] Level 3: District Water and Sanitation Mission - within 60 days. Final grievance at district level.
