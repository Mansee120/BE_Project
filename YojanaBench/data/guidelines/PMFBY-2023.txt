GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana
SCHEME ID: PMFBY-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: AGRICULTURE
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A crop insurance scheme that provides comprehensive risk cover to farmers against natural calamities, pests and diseases from pre-sowing to post-harvest, at a very low share of premium.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMFBY-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be a farmer with an insurable crop.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMFBY-E1] An enrolled farmer receives a sum insured of up to Rs. 1 lakh against crop loss.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMFBY-D1] (MANDATORY) Land Records
   [PMFBY-D2] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PMFBY-D3] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook
   [PMFBY-D4] (MANDATORY) Sowing Certificate

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMFBY-APL1] Level 1: Agriculture Officer - within 30 days. First grievance at the block level.
   [PMFBY-APL2] Level 2: District Agriculture Officer - within 60 days. Second grievance at district level.
   [PMFBY-APL3] Level 3: State Agriculture Commission - within 90 days. Final grievance at state level.
