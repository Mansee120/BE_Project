GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme
SCHEME ID: PMEGP-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: EMPLOYMENT
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   A credit-linked subsidy programme that supports the setting up of micro-enterprises in manufacturing and service sectors, generating self-employment opportunities in rural and urban areas.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMEGP-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 18 years of age or older.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMEGP-E1] An eligible beneficiary can set up a project with a maximum project cost of Rs. 10 lakh in the service sector.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMEGP-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PMEGP-D2] (MANDATORY) Project Report
   [PMEGP-D3] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMEGP-APL1] Level 1: KVIC or KVIB District Officer - within 30 days. First grievance at the district office.
   [PMEGP-APL2] Level 2: State Director (KVIC/KVIB) - within 60 days. Escalation at state level.
   [PMEGP-APL3] Level 3: Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises - within 90 days. Final grievance at the Ministry.
