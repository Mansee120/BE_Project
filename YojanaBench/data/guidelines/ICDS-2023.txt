GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Integrated Child Development Services
SCHEME ID: ICDS-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: NUTRITION
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   An early childhood care and development programme delivered through Anganwadi centres, providing supplementary nutrition, immunisation, health check-ups, referral services and pre-school education to children below six years and to mothers.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [ICDS-C1] (MANDATORY) The child must be 6 years of age or younger.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [ICDS-E1] Eligible children and mothers receive supplementary nutrition and pre-school services free of cost at the Anganwadi centre; there is no cash benefit.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [ICDS-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card of Child
   [ICDS-D2] (MANDATORY) Immunization Card
   [ICDS-D3] (MANDATORY) Address Proof

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [ICDS-APL1] Level 1: Anganwadi Worker - within 15 days. First grievance at the centre level.
   [ICDS-APL2] Level 2: Child Development Project Officer (CDPO) - within 30 days. Escalation at block level.
   [ICDS-APL3] Level 3: District Programme Officer - within 60 days. Final grievance at district level.
