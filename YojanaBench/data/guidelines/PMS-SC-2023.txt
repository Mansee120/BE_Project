GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste Students
SCHEME ID: PMS-SC-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: SCHOLARSHIP
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   Financial assistance to Scheduled Caste students studying at the post-matriculation level. Includes maintenance allowance (hosteller/day-scholar) and reimbursement of non-refundable fees, subject to parental income limits.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMS-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must belong to a Scheduled Caste (SC) category.
   [PMS-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must have passed matriculation (Class 10) and be pursuing post-matriculation studies.
   [PMS-C3] (MANDATORY) Parental/guardian annual income must not exceed Rs. 2,50,000.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMS-E1] Hostellers receive a maintenance allowance of Rs. 1,200 per month (Rs. 13,500 per year including book grant).
   [PMS-E2] Day scholars receive a maintenance allowance of Rs. 700 per month (Rs. 7,500 per year including book grant).

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMS-D1] (MANDATORY) Caste Certificate (SC)
   [PMS-D2] (MANDATORY) Income Certificate
   [PMS-D3] (MANDATORY) Class 10 Marksheet
   [PMS-D4] (MANDATORY) Fee Receipt / Admission Proof

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMS-APL1] Level 1: Institute Scholarship Nodal Officer - within 15 days. First-level grievance at the educational institute.
   [PMS-APL2] Level 2: State Scheduled Caste Development Department - within 45 days. Escalation to the state welfare department.
