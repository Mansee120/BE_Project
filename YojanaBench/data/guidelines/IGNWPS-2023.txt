GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Indira Gandhi National Widow Pension Scheme
SCHEME ID: IGNWPS-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: PENSION
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   Monthly pension for widows aged between 40 and 79 years belonging to Below Poverty Line (BPL) households. Enhanced amount for widows aged 80 years and above.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [IGNWPS-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be a widow.
   [IGNWPS-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must be 40 years of age or older.
   [IGNWPS-C3] (MANDATORY) The applicant must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [IGNWPS-E1] Widows aged 80 years and above receive a pension of Rs. 500 per month.
   [IGNWPS-E2] Widows aged 40 to 79 years receive a pension of Rs. 300 per month.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [IGNWPS-D1] (MANDATORY) Death Certificate of Husband
   [IGNWPS-D2] (MANDATORY) BPL Card
   [IGNWPS-D3] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [IGNWPS-D4] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [IGNWPS-APL1] Level 1: Block Development Officer (BDO) - within 30 days. First appeal at block level.
   [IGNWPS-APL2] Level 2: District Social Welfare Officer - within 60 days. Second appeal at district level.
   [IGNWPS-APL3] Level 3: State Social Welfare Commission - within 90 days. Final appeal at state level.
