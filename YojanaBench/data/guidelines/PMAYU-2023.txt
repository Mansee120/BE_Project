GOVERNMENT GUIDELINE DOCUMENT
SCHEME: Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban)
SCHEME ID: PMAYU-2023
LEVEL: CENTRAL   |   STATE: All India
CATEGORY: HOUSING
==============================================================================

1. OBJECTIVE
   An urban housing mission that provides central assistance and interest subsidy to economically weaker sections and lower income groups to enable them to build or buy a pucca house.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   [PMAYU-C1] (MANDATORY) The applicant's annual family income must not exceed Rs. 3 lakh.
   [PMAYU-C2] (MANDATORY) The applicant must not already own a pucca house.

3. ENTITLEMENT / BENEFIT RULES
   [PMAYU-E1] An eligible beneficiary receives central assistance of about Rs. 2.67 lakh towards the house.

4. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
   [PMAYU-D1] (MANDATORY) Aadhaar Card
   [PMAYU-D2] (MANDATORY) Income Certificate
   [PMAYU-D3] (MANDATORY) Bank Account Passbook
   [PMAYU-D4] (MANDATORY) Address Proof

5. APPLICATION & GRIEVANCE / APPEAL HIERARCHY
   [PMAYU-APL1] Level 1: Municipal Commissioner - within 30 days. First grievance at the urban local body.
   [PMAYU-APL2] Level 2: State Housing Board - within 60 days. Second grievance at state level.
   [PMAYU-APL3] Level 3: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs - within 90 days. Final grievance at the Ministry.
